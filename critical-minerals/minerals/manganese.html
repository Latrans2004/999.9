999.9/Critical minerals/Manganese

Cathode (LMO, NMC, LMFP)

Manganese

Mn

The low-concentration outlier among battery cathode inputs, and useful on this site mostly as a control. Manganese ore supply is spread across several large producers on three continents, so its index sits near the unconcentrated band even though the battery-grade purification step that follows is far narrower than the ore trade suggests.

What these numbers do not say

  • High-purity manganese sulphate monohydrate, the form batteries actually consume, has no distinct trade code and is far more concentrated than ore trade implies. The gap between the two is the point, not a flaw.
  • Both indices measure countries, not companies. Two mines in different countries owned by the same parent read here as two suppliers.
