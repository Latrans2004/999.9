Cathode energy density

Nickel

Ni

The volume metal of high-energy cathodes, and the one battery input with a genuinely large non-battery market in stainless steel. Indonesian output has grown fast enough over the last decade to move the index substantially on its own, which makes nickel the clearest example on this site of concentration as a recent event rather than a geological given.

Share of world mine production by country, from the USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries. This is where the material leaves the ground.

Share of world export value by reporting country, from UN Comtrade, covering Nickel ores and concentrates (2604.00) and unwrought nickel (7502.10, 7502.20). This captures processing and refining that mine statistics do not.

What these numbers do not say

  • Class 1 and Class 2 nickel are not substitutes for battery use but share trade codes, so the export index mixes battery-grade and stainless-grade material.
  • Both indices measure countries, not companies. Two mines in different countries owned by the same parent read here as two suppliers.
