Wiring and busbars

Copper

Cu

The benchmark. Copper is essential to every electrified system and is produced across a wide set of countries, so its index shows what an unconcentrated supply chain actually looks like on the same scale as the others. Reading cobalt or graphite against copper is the fastest way to see what those numbers mean.

Mine concentration

Share of world mine production by country, from the USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries. This is where the material leaves the ground.

Export concentration

What these numbers do not say

  • Copper is included as a low-concentration reference case. It is not on the 2022 USGS critical minerals list, though it appears on the 2025 draft list.
  • Both indices measure countries, not companies. Two mines in different countries owned by the same parent read here as two suppliers.
