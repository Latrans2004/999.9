Flow-battery electrolyte

Vanadium

V

The grid-storage entry on this list rather than a vehicle one. Vanadium redox flow batteries trade energy density for cycle life, which suits stationary storage, and vanadium supply is unusual in that most of it arrives as a co-product of steelmaking slag rather than from dedicated mines.

What these numbers do not say

  • The trade code aggregates vanadium with niobium and tantalum ores, so the export index for vanadium is materially less reliable than the others on this site and should be read as indicative only. The mine-side index is the one to trust here.
  • Most vanadium is recovered from steel slag and residues rather than mined directly, so mine-production shares understate the role of large steel producers.
  • Both indices measure countries, not companies. Two mines in different countries owned by the same parent read here as two suppliers.
