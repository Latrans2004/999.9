999.9/Critical minerals/Natural graphite

Anode

Natural graphite

C

The anode side of the cell, and the battery input with the single highest supply concentration of the eight. Synthetic graphite is a real substitute at a cost and emissions penalty, so the index here should be read as concentration of the cheap option rather than of the only option.

Mine concentration

Share of world mine production by country, from the USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries. This is where the material leaves the ground.

No data yet This index will appear once the pipeline completes a successful fetch from USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries.

Export concentration

Share of world export value by reporting country, from UN Comtrade, covering Natural graphite in powder or flakes (2504.10) and other natural graphite (2504.90). This captures processing and refining that mine statistics do not.

No data yet This index will appear once the pipeline completes a successful fetch from UN Comtrade.

What these numbers do not say

  • Synthetic graphite falls under a different trade code (3801.10) and is excluded, so the export index understates total anode-material supply.
  • Flake, vein and amorphous graphite command different prices and serve different end uses but share trade codes.
  • Both indices measure countries, not companies. Two mines in different countries owned by the same parent read here as two suppliers.
