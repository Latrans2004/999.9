What these numbers do not say

  • Synthetic graphite (3801.10) is excluded. Natural graphite trade codes do not identify battery grade; trade concentration remains under review.
  • Flake, vein and amorphous graphite command different prices and serve different end uses but share trade codes.
  • Fixed USGS MCS 2019–2026 editions cover 2017–2024. Estimates are retained; Other is excluded from country HHI. Rounded world totals can yield coverage capped at 100%. The 2018, 2020 and 2022 changes include differing source vintages and producer coverage; this is not a uniformly revised growth series.
  • Both indices measure countries, not companies. Two mines in different countries owned by the same parent read here as two suppliers.
