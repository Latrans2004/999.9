Two indices, not one

Why each mineral page carries a pair of numbers.

Mine concentration

Built from USGS world mine production by country. It answers where the material comes out of the ground, which is fixed by geology and slow to change. When a country is withheld from the USGS table, its share is reported as a coverage shortfall rather than guessed at.

Export concentration

Built from UN Comtrade annual export value by reporting country. It answers who actually ships the material, which captures refining and processing that mine statistics miss entirely. For several minerals here the two numbers are far apart, and that gap is the interesting part.
