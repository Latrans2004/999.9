Two indices, not one

Read the full method →

The mine index is built from USGS mine production by country; the export index from UN Comtrade export value by reporting country. Where the material leaves the ground and who actually ships it are different questions, and the gap between the two figures is where a processing chokepoint shows.

How to read the band
HHIBandReading
below 1,000UnconcentratedNo single supplier's absence reshapes the market.
1,000 – 1,800Moderately concentratedA handful of suppliers set the terms.
above 1,800Highly concentratedOne or two suppliers are the market.
