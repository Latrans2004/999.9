countries.geojson
=================

Country borders from Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/),
release v5.1.2 of https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector:

  geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson  sha256 6866c877d39cba9c357620878839b336d569f8c662d3cfab4cb1dbe2d39c977f
  geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson   sha256 3e458fc036ad0a66411f2c1e6cac49c5d7bfb81cb1123bc513b22511a2b7fdeb

Base layer is 110m; the 65 Admin 0 units that 110m does not contain are taken
from 50m. Only ADM0_A3 and ADMIN are kept and coordinates are rounded. Made
with tools/make_globe_borders.py.

Natural Earth is in the public domain. Made with Natural Earth.
